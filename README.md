# Juliana Rivera Camacho

**Molecular Machine Learning · Computational Drug Discovery · Protein–Ligand Data Analysis**

I am a scientist with a background in Biology and an M.Sc. in Molecular Life Sciences from **Universität Hamburg**, with research experience at **Universitätsklinikum Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE)**. I develop molecular machine-learning models and analyze protein–ligand data to investigate relationships between chemical structure, physicochemical properties and protein stability.

My work combines **Python, PyTorch and RDKit** with molecular representations, structural data analysis, model evaluation and scientific visualization.

## Featured research projects

### [LeadOptAI — molecular machine learning for lead-optimization research](https://github.com/julianariveracamacho/LeadOptAI-Overview)

Master’s thesis research at UKE investigating chemistry-aware molecular representation learning, molecular-property prediction and property-conditioned SMILES infilling.

- Developed a **bidirectional Transformer** with joint masked-token reconstruction and continuous property prediction.
- Implemented **functional-group masking with SMARTS** and **BRICS fragment masking** using RDKit.
- Built molecular-data, training and evaluation workflows; conducted baseline comparisons and masking experiments.
- Investigated six physicochemical descriptors: molecular weight, LogP, rotatable bonds, TPSA, hydrogen-bond donors and acceptors.

**Focus:** molecular representation learning · multi-task learning · cheminformatics · molecular-property modeling · localized molecular editing

The public repository provides a project overview and implementation map. Source code and unpublished experimental details remain private during publication preparation.

### [Protein–Ligand Stability Analysis — pharmacological-chaperone research](https://github.com/julianariveracamacho/protein-ligand-stability-analysis)

Research lab rotation at UKE analyzing processed protein-stability and protein–ligand data, primarily for phenylalanine hydroxylase (PAH), within a project involving PAH and GCDH.

- Developed **pandas/NumPy analysis workflows** for residue-level patterns, docking-pose comparisons and interaction summaries.
- Created **protein-domain, variant and binding-environment heatmaps**, interaction maps and compound-group distribution plots.
- Used collaborator-provided structural utilities for complex preparation, pocket extraction and interaction analysis.
- Explored interaction-pattern differences through contingency tables and statistical analysis.

**Focus:** structural bioinformatics · protein stability · protein–ligand interactions · docking-data analysis · scientific visualization

## Scientific approach

I am interested in models and analyses that answer clearly defined discovery questions. My research connects biological context with implementation, quantitative evaluation and careful interpretation.

LeadOptAI’s evaluated infilling task examines reconstruction and source-property preservation; optimization toward shifted property targets requires further validation. Similarly, docking-derived interactions provide hypotheses for experimental follow-up rather than proof of binding or enzyme rescue.
